Buying tips for mom's coffee gift

  • Know her brew method. Pod machine? Frother or K-Mini. Drip? Warmer, beans and mugs. Iced coffee fan? Glass cups and cold foam.
  • Splurge vs. budget: the Ember mug is the splurge; a mug warmer gives a similar 'hot coffee' result for much less.
  • Make it a set: mug + candle + book makes a lovely cozy-morning bundle.
