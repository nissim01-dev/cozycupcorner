Tips for teacher coffee gifts

  • Avoid more plain mugs — teachers often have dozens. Pick something functional (spill-proof, insulated, warming).
  • Group gifts: pooling $5 each from a few families easily covers a YETI or Contigo plus a coffee sampler.
  • Add a card: a note from the student is the part teachers keep forever.

FAQ

What is a good coffee gift for a teacher?

A spill-proof travel mug or an insulated tumbler, a mug warmer for their desk, or a coffee sampler to share in the staff room.

How much should I spend on a teacher's holiday gift?

Many families spend $10–$25; group class gifts often allow a premium item like a YETI mug.

Are mug warmers allowed in classrooms?

It depends on the school — some restrict small appliances, so a spill-proof insulated mug is the safest choice.
