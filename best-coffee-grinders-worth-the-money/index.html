Burr vs blade (and why it matters)

Blade grinders chop beans into uneven bits, so some over-extract (bitter) while others under-extract (sour). Burr grinders crush beans to a consistent size you can adjust. Espresso needs a grinder that goes very fine with small steps; drip and pour-over need evenness at medium settings. Pick by how the person brews.

FAQ

Is a burr grinder really worth it?

Yes — most coffee experts call it the single biggest upgrade for home coffee, because evenly ground fresh beans extract more cleanly and taste sweeter.

What is the best coffee grinder for espresso under $250?

The Breville Smart Grinder Pro, Fellow Opus 2 and Baratza Encore ESP are the most popular espresso-capable picks in that range.

Hand grinder or electric?

Electric is faster for daily use and espresso. Hand grinders are quieter, great for travel and give excellent quality for the price.
