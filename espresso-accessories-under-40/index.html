Buying guide: which espresso accessories first?

  1. Scale — measuring dose (in) and yield (out) is the foundation of consistent shots.
  2. WDT tool — a cheap tool that often fixes uneven extraction and spraying.
  3. Proper tamper — match your basket size exactly (51, 53, 54 or 58 mm are common).
  4. Knock box — makes cleanup quick, so you'll actually pull that second shot.
  5. Pitcher and cups — for milk drinks and presentation.

Fresh beans and a capable grinder still matter most; accessories help you get the best out of them.

FAQ

What size tamper do I need?

Check your machine's manual or measure the inside diameter of the portafilter basket. Common sizes are 51 mm, 53–54 mm and 58 mm.

Is a WDT tool worth it?

For most home espresso setups, yes — it's inexpensive and helps break up clumps for a more even extraction.
