How to pick a Secret Santa gift for a coworker

Stick to things that are useful at a desk, not too personal, and easy to regift if they don't drink coffee. Mug warmers and tumblers are the safest; socks and ornaments add a smile. If you know their drink — latte, iced coffee, pods — pick from that section.

FAQ

What's a good Secret Santa gift for a coffee lover under $25?

A mug warmer, an insulated tumbler, a handheld frother, glass iced coffee cups or a bag of strong coffee.

What if I don't know how they take their coffee?

A mug warmer or insulated tumbler works for any hot drink, including tea and cocoa.

What coffee gift works for white elephant?

Mug warmers and trendy tumblers are popular steals; funny coffee socks are a good 'laugh' gift.
