How to make a layered iced latte

  1. Add syrup to the bottom of the glass and stir with a splash of milk.
  2. Fill with ice, then add cold milk to about two-thirds.
  3. Slowly pour cold brew concentrate or chilled espresso over the back of a spoon so it floats in a separate layer.
  4. Top with cold foam (see our frother guide) and pop on the bamboo lid.

Buying guide: glass cups with lids

Size

16 oz is the classic "can glass"; 20 oz leaves more room for ice. Measure your fridge door or shelf if you prep drinks ahead.

Hot drinks?

Standard single-wall glass can get too hot to hold. Check the listing's heat guidance before pouring hot coffee, and avoid sudden temperature changes (like hot liquid in an ice-cold glass).

Care

Bamboo lids should be hand-washed and dried; glass straws need the included brushes. Replace silicone lid seals if they loosen.

Glass vs. insulated

Glass is for home and looks; for commuting, an insulated tumbler keeps ice from melting for hours.

FAQ

Are glass straws safe?

Glass straws made from thick, durable glass are widely used, but supervise young children and inspect straws for chips before use.

Can bamboo lids go in the dishwasher?

Generally, bamboo should be hand-washed and dried promptly to prevent warping or cracking — check the product listing.
