FAQ

What's the best gift for someone who works from home and loves coffee?

An Ember smart mug or a good mug warmer — both keep coffee hot during long calls.

Is a mug warmer as good as an Ember mug?

A warmer keeps an already-hot drink warm on the desk; an Ember mug holds a precise temperature and can leave the desk on battery.

What's a good WFH coffee gift under $30?

A mug warmer, a HARIO V60 starter set, a gooseneck kettle, a rechargeable frother or a coffee canister.
